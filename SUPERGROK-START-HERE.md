Read this file first, then look at the images in this order:
1. dark-v1/shell-C-full/00-core.png and 00-overview.png: CHOSEN direction, the grand arcade game shell (27 screens)
2. dark-v1/emblem-B-final/: CHOSEN Yuddha emblem "Liyawela Filigree" + intro sequence

Decisions: Yuddha (Ashtapadha, Maha Senaa, Yuddha Pro) is the main product; Daam Blitz and Oomi Somia are waiting-room mini games only.
Style: charcoal arcade game shell with bold red (Lanka / Hare 3:00) vs green (Ayodhya / Tortoise 5:00) and gold; Royal Palace intro; no guide-character face in the UI (she appears as a gold lotus seal).
Emblem: triangle holding a circle, a 6-ray star at the centre (no vertical line), Kandyan liyawela gold filigree.
Open: rename the "Yuddha Pro" rank or game; Maha Senaa / Yuddha Pro rules and clocks; Ashtapadha promotion rule; economy numbers.
